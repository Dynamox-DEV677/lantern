# Neighbour notes

Write these down **as they say them**: times, specifics, exact phrasing. Names are optional.
This file is the answer to *"how do you know it's real"*, the most important part of the submission.

Ask each neighbour two questions:

1. *"What do you do when the power goes?"*
2. *"How do you find out if it's just us or everyone?"*
